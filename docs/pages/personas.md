# Personas page

Persona Management lists the shared persona archetypes and shows how many master profiles are matched to each. The Personas tab is currently hidden from the top navigation, so the page is reached by its address (#/personas). Click a row to open that archetype's matched profiles.

## What you can do here
- **Filter**: Domain (All domains) and Status (All, Active, Inactive).
- **Read the KPIs**: Total Personas, Active / Inactive and Avg Confidence.
- **Read the charts**: Personas by Category and Personas by Risk Level.
- **Read the table**: Persona Archetype, Domain, Category, Total Matched Profiles, Status and Updated At.
- **Open matched profiles**: click a row, or the Total Matched Profiles link.
- **Add New Persona** (admins only): opens the create modal with Domain, Persona Code, Persona Name, Category, Summary, LLM Provider, LLM Model and an Active checkbox; Cancel or Create Persona.

## Sections of the page
- **Header and filters**: the title, Add New Persona and the Domain and Status filters.
- **KPI cards**: Total Personas, Active / Inactive and Avg Confidence.
- **Charts**: Personas by Category and Personas by Risk Level.
- **Personas table**: one row per archetype, with a Load more button in the footer.

## Common questions
- **What is a persona archetype?** A shared persona definition that profiles are matched against, not a single customer.
- **How do I see who matches a persona?** Click the row or the Total Matched Profiles link.
- **How do I create a persona?** Add New Persona (admins only).
- **Why is Avg Confidence 0%?** No profiles have been matched to these archetypes yet.
- **What does Active mean?** The archetype is eligible for new profile matching.
